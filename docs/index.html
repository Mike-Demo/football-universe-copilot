    Pitch telemetry — Voronoi zones & expected threat

    Press Play — the snapshot follows the latest key moment. It is fully interactive: click a ringed marker to jump the replay to that moment, tap a player dot to identify the slot, tap the labelled ball carrier to find them in the knowledge graph, or tap any grass to read its xT.

    · xT = our documented expected-threat surface (distance/angle to goal); shot xG = 2.2 × xT at the shot location, capped at 0.95. Both are simple transparent models — formulas on the tech page.

    Poisson what-if — Tactical Lab

    Drag the expected-goals rates (λ) and the outcome probabilities recompute live from two independent Poisson distributions. What-if only — it never changes a simulated result.
