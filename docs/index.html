          Watch: the whole idea in two minutes

          New here? Welcome in. This page is a football companion. It watches a match with you and explains what is happening, and why it matters, in words anyone can follow. No football brain required. No computer brain required either. This short video tells the whole story — or step through it below, at your own pace.

          The two-steps-apart story

          You might think Nicolas Anelka and Erling Haaland have nothing to do with each other. They played in different times and never shared a pitch. But this page keeps a big map of who played where, and on that map the two of them are only two steps apart: Anelka played for Manchester City, and so did Haaland. It works like friends of friends. You may never have met someone, yet you share a friend. Football is full of hidden links like that, and finding them is half the fun here.

          Meet the four helpers

          Four helpers share the work, like a little team behind the curtain:

          • The reader watches the match closely and writes down everything that happens: every pass, shot, and goal.
          • The memory remembers how all the players and clubs connect to each other, going back years.
          • The storyteller turns those notes into the story of the match, as it unfolds.
          • The shaper adjusts the story to fit you. Want it short and simple? Following one favourite player? Cheering for one club? The same match, retold your way.

          A recap written on the spot

          In the match area there is a button marked Ask Azure. Tap it, and a real AI writes a fresh recap of the match for you, right there and then. Nothing is copied from a script. And this page always tells you where the words came from, so you can see whether a recap was written fresh for you or filled in with a ready-made summary. No pretending, ever.

          Two fun extras

          Chaos Arcade turns the same match into a silly arcade game, with confetti, sound effects, and daft camera angles. Pride Matchday dresses the same match in rainbows, because football is for everyone. Same match underneath, just dressed up for fun.

          Chaos Arcade is a hidden extra: tap the ball at the top of the page 7 times (or use the Konami code) once you are back on the page.

          One honest note

          The matches on this page are made up. A computer invented them for practice, so no real results are on the line. The players and clubs are real, and so are the links between them. The games themselves never actually happened.
